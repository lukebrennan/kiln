# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages. Delegated: plain CSS and a small vanilla JS file replace the Tailwind CDN script.

## Users

Primary visitor is whoever the site's author shows it to: prospective clients, employers and peers judging web design craft. Secondary, in the fiction: Liverpool locals deciding whether to walk into Kiln.

## Product Purpose

Kiln is a concept coffee roastery and bakery on a fictional Merchant Lane in Liverpool. The site exists as a design showpiece. Success means a visitor leaves impressed by the craft and still understands what Kiln is, what it serves, and when and where to find it.

## Positioning

An independent roastery that roasts on site in small weekly batches and bakes sourdough daily. The roasting itself (the heat, the timing, the batch log) is the mechanism a generic café site cannot truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

Visited mostly on phones by people on the move; desktop for the portfolio audience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static single page. No ordering, booking or checkout.
- All business facts (address, hours, prices, coffees, email) are placeholders and may be rewritten freely. They must be flagged as such to the owner when handed over.
- Photography: keep Unsplash stock for now and add more when needed. Real photos may replace them later.

## Brand Commitments

Industrial look: dark colours with an orange accent. Name is Kiln.

## Evidence on Hand

No real testimonials, press, customers or photography. Do not fabricate reviews or awards.

## Product Principles

1. Plain and readable first: menu, hours and location are findable in seconds.
2. Design craft is the proof, so every detail is authored, not templated.
3. Practical facts (hours, location, menu) stay findable within seconds.
4. Placeholder content is honest about being placeholder.
