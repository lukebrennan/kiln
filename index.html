Why Kiln

What you drink and eat here was made hours ago, not days. That is the whole idea.

  • Roasted weeklySmall batches in the front window, so the beans on the shelf are days old, not weeks.
  • Baked dailyLaminated and proved overnight, out of the oven by seven.
  • Sourced directSingle-origin lots from producers we can name, listed on every bag.
